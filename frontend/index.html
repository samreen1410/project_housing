Greater Vancouver

Where the mountains meet the sea

Metro Vancouver sits on Canada's west coast, tucked between the Coast Mountains and the Pacific Ocean in British Columbia. It is known for its mountains, beaches, and rainforest all within reach of downtown, a film and tech industry that has earned Vancouver the nickname Hollywood North, and one of the most multicultural populations in the country. The region is made up of several distinct cities rather than one, each with its own character and its own rental market, which is exactly why comparing them matters.

About me

Why I built this

I am Samreen, a fourth year Science student at UBC. I have lived in Metro Vancouver for about five years now, and I genuinely love it here. The mountains are visible from so many parts of the city that I still stop to look some mornings, and having the ocean, a rainforest, and a ski hill all within a short drive of downtown still does not feel normal in the best way. I love how many different cultures and cuisines live side by side here, and how much the city changes character from one neighbourhood to the next. At the same time, figuring out where I could actually afford to live, once rent, groceries, and getting around were all added up, was a real problem I ran into myself. I built this tool to make those numbers easier to see and compare, using real public data instead of guesswork.
